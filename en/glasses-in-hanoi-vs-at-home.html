What is the same wherever you buy

More than most people expect.

  • The lens brands. Essilor and ZEISS are global manufacturers. A Varilux or a ZEISS SmartLife lens sold in Hanoi is the same product sold in Munich or Melbourne, carrying the same authenticity markings on the packaging.
  • The prescription notation. Sphere, cylinder, axis, addition and pupillary distance are written the same way everywhere. There is nothing to translate.
  • The physics. A higher-index material makes a strong prescription thinner in Hanoi exactly as it does at home.

So the question is not whether the lenses are different. It is what else is in the price.

What can go wrong — and how to check

This is the part we would want a member of our own family to read.

A rushed eye test

An autorefractor gives a machine reading in seconds. It is a starting point, not a prescription. A proper refraction takes longer and involves you answering which of two lenses looks clearer.

What to ask: for a full refraction test, not only the machine reading. Ours takes as long as it takes and is free either way.

A prescription copied rather than measured

Hand over an old prescription and if nobody re-measures, you get glasses matched to the eyes you had two years ago.

What to ask: to be measured anyway, and for the new numbers in writing to take home.

No way to get an adjustment after you leave

Glasses shift with wear. Ours are adjusted free for life, which is worth nothing to you once you are on a plane.

What to do: come back before you fly and have the fit checked once more. It takes five minutes and it is the single most useful thing a traveller can do.

Lenses that are not what was sold

What to ask: to see the lens packaging with its authenticity markings before the lens is cut. We show it as a matter of course; anywhere that will not is answering the question for you.

Common questions

Is it worth buying glasses in Vietnam?
It depends on what you need and how long you are staying. Single-vision glasses are usually ready the same day and cost less than in most high-income countries. Essilor or ZEISS progressives take three to four days because they are surfaced at the manufacturer's lab, so they suit a longer stay. The lens brands are the same global ones; what differs is the price of the labour and the frame.
Are glasses in Vietnam good quality?
Quality depends on the lens, the measurement and the fitting, not on the country. Essilor and ZEISS lenses sold here are the same products sold elsewhere and carry the manufacturer's authenticity markings. Ask to see the lens packaging before the lens is cut, and ask which lens design and index you are being quoted.
What can go wrong buying glasses abroad?
The three common problems are a rushed eye test, a prescription transcribed rather than measured, and having no way to get an adjustment after you fly home. Ask for a full refraction test, take the written prescription with you, and have the fit checked before you leave the country.
Should I bring my own frame to Vietnam?
You can. Bring a frame you are attached to and we will tell you honestly whether it will take the lenses your prescription needs. Bringing your current glasses is useful in any case.
How much do glasses cost in Hanoi?
A finished single-vision pair of frame and lenses runs from 850,000₫ to 3,500,000₫ here. An Essilor blue-light filtering lens is 1,480,000₫. Visitors from abroad typically spend between 1,500,000₫ and 4,000,000₫, because they more often choose photochromic, progressive or branded lenses.
