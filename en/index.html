Start with what happened

You do not need to know any lens vocabulary. Find the line below that sounds like your situation, then tell us that much when you walk in.

I need glasses fast and I am leaving Hanoi soon
Say your departure date first. Single-vision lenses are held in stock and are normally cut, fitted and ready the same day. We would rather tell you a lens will not be finished in time than take the order and hope.
I broke or lost my glasses on this trip
Bring the broken pair if you still have it, since the old lenses give us a reference to start from. Then we test your eyes properly, free, and you choose from around a thousand frames. Most people in this situation walk out the same day.
I just want my eyes checked
That is a whole visit on its own here, and it costs nothing. A full refraction test takes 20 to 30 minutes, you get your numbers to keep, and there is no expectation that you buy anything. We test rather than copy the prescription off your current lenses, because those two are not the same thing.
I want new lenses in the frame I already own
You are welcome to bring it. We check the frame first and say plainly if it is too brittle or too worn to survive a re-fit, before anything is cut.
I need progressives, sunglasses with my prescription, or a strong prescription
Standard progressives take about 24 hours; Essilor and ZEISS progressives are cut at the lab and take three to four days. Photochromic lenses, prescription sunglasses and blue-light filtering coatings are all made here too. Bring your departure date to this conversation as well.
I am only free in the evening
We are open until 21:30, every day of the week, including weekends and holidays. If your daylight hours belong to the city, come after them. The eye test at 20:00 is the same test as the one at 10:00.

What we do

  • Free eye test, a full refraction test whether or not you buy. Re-checks stay free for as long as you wear the pair.
  • Lenses: single vision, progressive (multifocal), blue-light filtering, photochromic and prescription sunglasses, from Essilor, ZEISS and other stocked manufacturers.

If you are visiting Hanoi

Most of the people who walk in here are visitors, so the answers below are the questions we are actually asked at the counter. If you are on a short trip, tell us the date you leave Hanoi when you arrive. We will only recommend lenses that can be finished and fitted before you go. There are 22 more answers on the questions page if yours is not below.

I am only in Hanoi for a few days. Can I get glasses in time?
Usually yes. Single-vision lenses are held in stock and are normally cut, fitted and ready the same day. Standard progressives take about 24 hours. Essilor and ZEISS progressives are cut at the lab and take three to four days, so those need a little more room in your trip. Tell us your departure date and we will say plainly whether a lens fits or not.
Do I need to bring a prescription from home?
No. The eye test here is free, takes about 20 to 30 minutes, and you are not expected to buy afterwards. If you have a current prescription or your present glasses, bring them, it gives us a starting point. We will still test rather than copy a prescription that is more than a year old, because the numbers move.
How much does a pair cost?
A finished single-vision pair, frame and lenses together, runs from 850,000₫ to 3,500,000₫. As one concrete example, an Essilor blue-light filtering lens is 1,480,000₫ a pair. Progressive, photochromic and branded lenses cost more, and we will show you the price of each option before anything is cut.
What actually changes when a lens costs more?
Three things, in plain terms. Thickness: a higher index lens is thinner and lighter at the same prescription, which matters most above about 3 dioptres. Coatings: anti-reflective, scratch-resistant and blue-light filtering layers are what you pay for on top of the plain lens. Design: a progressive lens has a wider, smoother reading zone the more it costs. On blue-light filtering we will say what the evidence says: a 2023 Cochrane review found no clear benefit for eye strain, and the American Academy of Ophthalmology puts screen fatigue down mainly to blinking less. The anti-reflective layer does cut glare, which many people do notice. It treats no eye condition.
Do the staff speak English?
Yes, on every shift, including evenings. The eye test, the lens explanation and the fitting all happen in English if you prefer.
Can I pay by card, and can I bring my own frame?
Card and cash are both accepted. You are welcome to bring a frame you already own and have new lenses fitted into it, as long as the frame is sound enough to take them.
Can you deliver to my hotel, or ship the glasses to me at home?
Delivery within inner Hanoi is free, hotels included. If you fly out before the pair is ready, international shipping can be arranged; we confirm the destination, the cost and a realistic delivery time before you order.
Will anything here fit a larger face?
Yes. Among the thousand or so frames in store there are several sizes, including wider fronts and higher bridges. This is the question visitors ask most nervously and it is the one most easily settled by trying a few on.
What if the glasses need adjusting after I have left?
Adjustments, cleaning and new nose pads are free here for as long as you own the pair, so come back any time you are in Hanoi. A genuine manufacturing fault is replaced. For small tweaks once you are home, any optician can adjust the fit; the frames are standard.
