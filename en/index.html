What we do

  • Free eye test — a full refraction test, whether or not you buy. Re-checks stay free for as long as you wear the pair.
  • Lenses — single vision, progressive (multifocal), blue-light filtering, photochromic and prescription sunglasses, from Essilor, ZEISS and other stocked manufacturers.
  • Frames — roughly a thousand in store: acetate, flexible plastic, metal and titanium, including a large selection of Korean designs. You are welcome to bring your own frame.
  • Aftercare — adjustments, cleaning and new nose pads are free for as long as you own the pair. A genuine manufacturing fault is replaced.
