Chemi Lenses: Stock or Custom-Ground, A-ONE or A-PLUS

Written by Alvin · Manh Long Optical, 147A Le Duan, Cua Nam, Hanoi · Updated 11 September 2026

In short: Chemi lenses come two ways: stock lenses, cut same-day, and custom-ground lenses, surfaced to your exact prescription at a lab, taking 10-15 days. At the same index and tier, a custom-ground lens costs roughly 2.6 to 6.7 times more than a stock one. If your prescription falls inside the stock range, a stock lens does the job; custom-ground is for high prescriptions, significant astigmatism, or an axis outside that range.

At Chemi, a stock lens at 1.67 index (the U2 tier, 1,186,000-1,304,000 VND) costs less than a custom-ground lens at 1.56 index (the U1 tier, 1,400,000 VND). With Chemi lenses, how the lens is made pushes the price up more than the index does. This page walks through the shop's Chemi price sheet, September 2026.

U1, U2, U6 are the tier names printed on the Chemi price sheet. A higher number means a higher price. The difference is in the coating — ask staff when choosing.

2. Same 1.67 index, so why is the price gap almost three times?

Both are Chemi 1.67 aspheric U2: the stock lens is 1,186,000 VND, the custom-ground version is 3,468,000 VND. The difference is in how the lens is made, not the raw material.

Stock lenses

The factory mass-produces lenses at common prescription steps and stocks them in the warehouse. Once you're measured, we pull the matching pair off the shelf and fit it — same-day cutting. This only works when your prescription falls within the brand's stocked range.

Custom-ground lenses

These start as a semi-finished blank. Once your prescription is in hand, the lab surfaces one face of the lens to your exact sphere, cylinder and axis. Used when your prescription is high, your astigmatism is significant, or your axis falls outside the stock range. The edge of the lens stays clearer for these larger deviations. It has to wait for the lab: 10-15 days.

Which one you need

If your prescription sits within the common range, a stock lens does exactly the job it's meant to do. Paying three times more for a custom-ground lens in that case pays for something you don't need. After your eye test, we'll tell you which group your prescription falls into.

4. What's the difference between A-ONE and A-PLUS?

Both are Chemi progressives: A-ONE is a conventional progressive design, A-PLUS is a free-form progressive. Free-form means the lens surface is ground point-by-point by a digitally controlled machine, rather than relying on a pre-molded progressive surface on the blank.

The price sheet doesn't state how much wider or smoother A-PLUS is than A-ONE. This page doesn't put a number on it.

5. Who shouldn't choose Chemi lenses?

Anyone who needs glasses within a few days whose prescription falls outside the stock range. Chemi custom-ground lenses take 10-15 days; if you're about to leave Hanoi, mention your departure date as soon as you arrive so staff only suggest options that can be finished in time.

Anyone who needs a high-index photochromic lens, or 1.70 or 1.76 index. Chemi photochromic lenses on the price sheet are only available at 1.56, and Chemi doesn't offer 1.70 or 1.76. Those two are covered by Tokai lenses. To compare Chemi against the other four brands at the same index, see lens brands compared.
