Manh Long Optical: 25 Years of Frames, Wholesale and Retail, Lens Partner to Essilor, Zeiss, Nikon

Written by Alvin · Manh Long Optical, 147A Le Duan, Cua Nam, Hanoi · Updated 11 September 2026

In short: Manh Long Optical has supplied frames wholesale and retail since the early 2000s, now in its 25th year, and is a partner of the international lens brands Essilor, Carl Zeiss and Nikon. The shop covers eyewear needs in one place — from contact lenses and progressives to single-vision and specialty lenses, plus swim goggles and sports glasses — fitted and cut on modern equipment by experienced technicians, regularly serving international customers.

25 Years Supplying Frames, Wholesale and Retail

Manh Long Optical has been in the eyewear business since the early 2000s. Over 25 years, the shop has built its business on two parallel tracks: supplying frames wholesale to other retailers, and selling and fitting glasses directly to individual customers coming in for an eye test or a new pair of sunglasses.

This dual model gives the shop a volume and variety of frames that a retail-only shop typically doesn't have — useful whether you're an individual customer comparing styles, or another shop needing to stock up on frames.

International Lens Partner: Essilor, Carl Zeiss, Nikon

Manh Long Optical fits prescription lenses from three major names in the industry: Essilor, Carl Zeiss and Nikon. New lines from these brands arrive regularly, for customers who want the latest lens technology rather than older stock.

Current new arrivals include Essilor Eyezen, Crizal Rock, Transitions, Essentials and Varilux, alongside Zeiss ClearView, Lens 2.0, DriveSafe and Office.

Every Eyewear Need in One Place

Beyond standard prescription glasses, the shop also carries supporting and specialty products that usually mean visiting several different places:

  • Contact lenses — for myopia, astigmatism, colored lenses, and circle lenses
  • Progressive, bifocal and single-vision lenses
  • Swim goggles and prescription sports glasses

Specialty Lenses for Specific Needs

For customers with specific needs — computer work, night driving, or simply wanting a tinted lens — the shop carries a range of specialty lenses:

  • Tinted prescription lenses
  • UV-blocking lenses
  • Blue-light-filtering lenses
  • Photochromic (light-adaptive) lenses
  • Night-driving lenses
  • Lenses for computer use and office work

Modern Equipment, Experienced Technicians, International Customers

Manh Long Optical regularly serves international customers, so its service standard has to meet varied expectations from country to country. The shop invests in modern measuring and fitting equipment, with a technician team whose skills match it — all aimed at protecting your eye health, not just producing a pair of glasses.
