Mass-produced lenses are designed around average numbers. SmartLife Individual reverses that: the lens is built from your own face, your own frame, and your own viewing habits — Zeiss's answer to a digital life spent constantly shifting focus.

What is Zeiss SmartLife Individual?

Zeiss SmartLife Individual is the most personalized lens in Carl Zeiss's SmartLife family. Unlike mass-produced lenses built on average measurements, SmartLife Individual is designed and made to order based on your own anatomical measurements and actual viewing habits.

Combining technology optimized for eye movement in the digital era with deep personalization gives the lens sharp, natural vision down to the millimeter. Whether you wear single-vision, near-vision-support or progressive lenses, Zeiss SmartLife Individual is calculated specifically to your own eye measurements.

The personalization technologies built into Zeiss SmartLife Individual

IndividualFit & FrameFit technology: every frame model has a different shape, size and curve. This technology analyzes the precise dimensions of your chosen frame to adjust how the vision zones are distributed across the lens, maximizing the wide field of view without being constrained by the frame style.

FaceFit technology: optimizes the lens based on three real anatomical measurements — the pantoscopic tilt of the frame, the vertex distance from the lens to the front of the cornea, and the frame's wrap angle. This fine-tuning ensures the lens's optical center lines up exactly with your eye's natural viewing axis. SmartView & Age-Fit technology combines research into eye physiology by age with how pupil diameter changes under different lighting conditions, optimizing how much light reaches the eye for sharp, accurate vision even in low light.

Standout benefits for the wearer

The biggest benefit of Zeiss SmartLife Individual is that it largely eliminates visual fatigue. Made to measure for your exact head and eye movement habits, wearers don't get edge distortion or that swaying sensation when walking. Shifting focus between far, intermediate and near feels as smooth as natural, unaided vision.

It also lets you keep a comfortable head and neck posture throughout a long workday. You don't need to tilt your head back or turn it to find a clear angle on your phone or computer screen. Shoulder, neck and eye-socket fatigue by the end of the day is largely resolved. The lens also carries full eye-protection technology such as Zeiss UVProtect, blocking 100% of harmful UV, plus smart blue-light filtering to help protect the retina from digital screens.

Who should invest in Zeiss SmartLife Individual lenses?

Zeiss SmartLife Individual is the ideal option for anyone seeking the sharpest possible result and unwilling to compromise on visual quality: people with a complex prescription, high astigmatism, or a significant difference in pupillary distance between the two eyes.

It also suits business owners, managers and professionals working in roles that demand high focus and constant movement; people who struggle to adapt to standard progressives because of a specific wearing posture or sensitivity to edge distortion; and anyone who wants a made-to-measure lens fully matched to the premium frame they've chosen.

Care and cleaning for Zeiss SmartLife Individual lenses

To keep the optical quality and the premium nano-coatings on Zeiss SmartLife Individual performing well, build a proper cleaning routine. Clean the lens daily with a dedicated lens spray and the included microfiber cloth. Avoid a shirt hem, dry tissue or harsh cleaning chemicals, since these scratch the lens surface easily.

When not wearing them, always store the glasses in a hard protective case to avoid physical knocks. Keep the lenses away from high heat sources such as a motorbike seat box, a car dashboard in strong sun, or a sauna, since high temperatures can affect how long the optical coatings last.

Common questions

How is SmartLife Individual different from a standard SmartLife lens?
The lens is made specifically to your own face and frame measurements — frame tilt, vertex distance, frame curve — instead of using an average value across a production batch.
Do I need a complex prescription to benefit from SmartLife Individual?
No — this lens also suits people who move around and shift focus constantly through the day, or who struggle to adapt to a standard progressive, regardless of whether their prescription is simple or complex.
How long does it take to get SmartLife Individual lenses?
Measurements are taken in-store on the frame you've chosen, then sent to the Zeiss factory for made-to-order manufacturing — ask Manh Long Optical for the specific delivery timeline when you order.
Does SmartLife Individual work with any frame?
Yes — IndividualFit and FrameFit technology is exactly what adjusts the lens design to match the shape and curve of the frame you've chosen.
Should I get SmartLife Individual with a simple prescription?
It's still worth considering if you move around and shift focus constantly through the day — the personalization targets that comfort, not just the prescription itself.
Can SmartLife Individual be made as a progressive lens?
Yes — this layer of personalization applies to single-vision, near-vision-support and progressive lenses alike.
What if I switch to a different frame later?
Since FrameFit and FaceFit are measured for one specific frame, switching to a frame with a notably different shape usually means a new measurement and a new lens.
