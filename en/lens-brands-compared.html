Lens brands compared: Essilor, ZEISS, Tokai, Chemi and Elements in Hanoi

Reviewed by Alvin, Store Manager · Manh Long Optical, 147A Le Duan, Cua Nam, Hanoi · Updated 10 September 2026

In short: Comparing the five lens brands on our Hanoi price list, none comes out ahead on every line: at the same index, Chemi starts lower than the other four brands at 1.56, 1.60, 1.67 and 1.74. Tokai offers 1.70 and 1.76, two indices Essilor and ZEISS do not have on our list; of these five brands, Elements is the one with 1.61, and its photochromic 1.67 starts at 2,480,000₫, below the 1.67 photochromic lenses from Essilor and ZEISS. Essilor costs less than ZEISS from 1.56 to 1.67, while ZEISS 1.74 goes to -20.00 and ZEISS has photochromic lenses up to 1.74.

“What is the difference between a cheap lens and an expensive one?” It is one of the questions visitors actually ask at our counter. On a single price list, a pair of 1.67 lenses runs from 700,000₫ to 4,500,000₫ depending on brand and how the lens is made. This page compares the five brands we sell using the numbers on that list, not reputation.

The brand names and index numbers are the same worldwide, so you can hold these figures against a quote from home. Prices are in Vietnamese đồng; your phone's currency converter gives today's figure.

3. What does the extra money buy?

On our list the gap comes from three things you can read off it: the prescription range the maker covers, how the lens is made (stock or made to order), and the coating version. The list does not grade optical quality, so neither does this page.

Range. ZEISS ClearView DuraVision Gold 1.74 covers short-sight to -20.00 with astigmatism to 6.00. Essilor Blue UV Capture 1.74 goes to -18.00. Chemi U2 1.74 in stock covers -1.00 to -10.00 with astigmatism to 4.00, and Chemi U6 1.74 in stock goes to -15.00. The stronger the prescription, the shorter the list of brands that can make it.

How it is made. A Chemi 1.67 U2 stock lens is 1,186,000₫ to 1,304,000₫ a pair. The same line made to your prescription at the lab is 3,468,000₫, about 2.7 to 2.9 times more, and takes 10 to 15 days.

Coating version. Elements 1.67 is 1,480,000₫ to 1,580,000₫ in the standard version and 1,680,000₫ to 1,880,000₫ in Night AR. Chemi splits its lines into U1, U2 and U6. Different coating, different price.

On blue-light filtering, the same applies to every brand: what it does is cut glare and reflections from screens. A 2023 Cochrane systematic review found no evidence that it reduces eye strain, and the American Academy of Ophthalmology puts screen strain down mainly to blinking less.

6. What does a whole pair cost, frame included?

A finished single-vision pair, frame and lenses together, runs from 850,000₫ to 3,500,000₫. The 850,000₫ pair uses a standard lens, not one of the five brands above.

The other prices on this page cover the lenses alone. Visitors from abroad usually spend between 1,500,000₫ and 4,000,000₫ on a pair, often because they choose photochromic, progressive or branded lenses. You get the full figure before anything is cut, and nothing is added later. Cards are accepted.

7. Who should not choose by brand name?

Mild prescriptions. Under 3 dioptres we suggest 1.60, where Chemi starts at 385,000₫ and ZEISS is 2,750,000₫. Same index, same thickness by the formula.

Anyone short on time whose prescription is outside the stock range. Chemi made-to-order lenses take 10 to 15 days and Essilor or ZEISS progressives three to four. And anyone hoping an expensive lens will make them see sharper: a premium lens does not fix a wrong prescription. Get the eye test right first.

Common questions

Which lens brand should I choose in Hanoi?
It depends on your prescription, budget and how long you are staying, because on our price list no brand comes out ahead on every line. Chemi starts lower than the other four at 1.56, 1.60, 1.67 and 1.74; Tokai offers 1.70 and 1.76; Elements offers 1.61; Essilor costs less than ZEISS from 1.56 to 1.67, and ZEISS 1.74 goes to -20.00.
Is Tokai the most expensive lens brand you sell?
No. The highest Tokai price at our shop is the SERENO IS progressive at 11,600,000₫, and Tokai 1.76 single vision tops out at 9,300,000₫. On the same list, ZEISS ClearView 1.74 is 12,280,000₫, Essilor Blue UV Capture 1.74 is 12,380,000₫ and ZEISS PhotoFusion 1.74 is 16,380,000₫.
Can I get branded lenses before I leave Hanoi?
Single-vision lenses in stock are usually cut the same day, whichever brand. Essilor and ZEISS progressives take three to four days and Chemi lenses made to order take 10 to 15 days, so tell us your departure date when you walk in.
Do these lens prices include a frame?
No. They are September 2026 prices for a pair of lenses without a frame, and the final price is confirmed after your eye test. A finished single-vision pair, frame and lenses together, runs from 850,000₫ to 3,500,000₫.
