How we publish feedback

  • Only real customers who bought glasses here. This page uses no models, no AI-generated people and no stock photos.
  • A customer photo goes up only with that person's permission. To have yours removed, message us on WhatsApp or Zalo, 0777 986 999.
  • Quotes come from Google, with the reviewer's name exactly as Google shows it and a link to the original.
  • Customer photos and Google reviews are kept apart. We pair a photo with a review only after confirming it is the same person.
  • We do not trade gifts for reviews, and we do not delete the bad ones.
