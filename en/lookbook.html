The four shapes in this lookbook

Small oval, dark red acetate. A small rim covers less of the face and suits stronger prescriptions, because a smaller lens is thinner at the edge. Acetate carries deep colour and some thickness, so it reads clearly on the face.

Round metal, slim gold rim. A classic shape; the thin rim keeps it light and quiet. A round line softens faces with angles at the jaw and brow.

Gold oval metal, slim temples. A neutral shape that works on many faces. Metal is lighter than acetate, so it presses less on the ears and nose over a long day.

Red acetate cat-eye. The outer corners lift, drawing the eye toward the temples. For people who want the frame to be the point, not only a tool for seeing.
