Progressive Glasses in Hanoi

Written by Alvin · Manh Long Optical · 147A Le Duan, Cua Nam, Hanoi · Updated 11 October 2026

In short: progressive (multifocal) glasses are made to measure, so the lens you choose decides how long you wait. At 147A Le Duan, Chemi Magic Form progressives take about 24 hours, Essilor progressives four to five days and ZEISS progressives seven to nine. Lenses start at 3,200,000₫ a pair. The eye test is free, and the fitting is measured on the frame you pick.

Progressives put distance, middle and reading vision in one lens, with no visible line. That convenience depends on two things more than the brand name: an up-to-date prescription, and measurements taken on the exact frame you will wear. Both happen here, in one visit.

Why the fitting matters more than the brand

A progressive lens has a narrow corridor between distance and reading. If it sits too high, too low or off-centre, you end up tilting your head to find the clear spot, whatever the lens cost. So the order here is:

  1. A free eye test, 20 to 30 minutes: auto-refraction first, then a trial frame, never cutting straight from a machine reading.
  2. You choose the frame.
  3. Pupillary distance and fitting height are measured on that frame, as you wear it.
  4. After you collect, if you have a spare day, wear them for an evening and come back to have the fit adjusted.

Most first-time wearers adjust within a few days to two weeks. Wear them all day from day one rather than switching back to old glasses. If something still feels off after about a week, come back: re-measuring and adjusting is free for as long as you own the pair. For how the Varilux designs differ, read Multifocal lenses explained.
