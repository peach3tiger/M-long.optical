3. Products and services

  • Frames: around 1,000 in store, in acetate, flexible plastic, metal and titanium, including wide sizes and higher nose bridges.
  • Lenses: single vision, progressive (multifocal), blue-light filtering, photochromic, and prescription sunglass lenses. Manh Long Optical is an official partner of Essilor, ZEISS and Tokai, and also carries lenses from other manufacturers.
  • Sunglasses: non-prescription sunglasses, polarized sunglasses (non-prescription only), and prescription sunglasses.
  • Eye tests: a free refraction test, whether or not you buy.
  • Lenses in your own frame: the fitting is free, the lenses are charged at lens price. We check the frame before accepting it.
  • Aftercare: eye tests, prescription re-checks, frame adjustment, cleaning and new nose pads are free for life, including on glasses bought elsewhere.

4. Eye tests and prescriptions

  • An eye test in store usually takes 20 to 30 minutes: an automatic refraction, then a trial frame and a check of both eyes working together.
  • A refraction test for glasses is not a medical eye examination. If we see anything unusual, or a young child cannot cooperate with the test, we will advise you to see a hospital eye specialist.
  • If you bring a prescription from home, we still measure and compare before making your glasses.
  • We do not make glasses remotely from a prescription that is more than 12 months old. Come in and we will measure again; the test is always free.

6. Prices and offers

  • Prices are in Vietnamese dong. Website prices are guide prices, usually shown as "from".
  • The final price of prescription glasses is set after the eye test, because it depends on your prescription, lens type, lens index and frame.
  • Each offer has its own conditions and end date, shown on the Offers page and in store.

7. Orders and made-to-order lenses

An order is agreed once you and our staff have settled the frame, the lens line and features, your eye measurements, the total price and the collection date. There are three kinds of product:

  • Ready-made: frames without lenses, non-prescription sunglasses, accessories.
  • Prescription lenses cut from stock: most single-vision glasses, usually ready the same day.
  • Lenses made to order: progressives (standard about 24 hours; premium Essilor four to five days; premium ZEISS seven to nine days), photochromic lenses in difficult prescriptions, high powers and strong astigmatism. For special prescriptions, we give you a date after checking the prescription.

Made-to-order lenses go into production for your measurements as soon as the order is agreed, so please check the details before you agree.
