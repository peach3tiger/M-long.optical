# Figma AI Prompt — M-LonG.Optical (Korean Minimal Eyewear Brand)

## PROMPT (copy nguyên đoạn này)

Design a minimalist Korean eyewear e-commerce website called **"M-LonG.Optical"** — inspired by Seoul eyewear brands like RIETI and Gentle Monster's clean editorial e-commerce aesthetic.

**Brand concept:** Contemporary Korean optical brand. Tagline: *"Own your light."* Mood: quiet luxury, editorial fashion magazine, generous whitespace, black-and-white base with one deep indigo accent. Tone of voice: short poetic English lines paired with small Korean body text.

**Design system:**
- **Colors:** Background pure white `#FFFFFF`; primary text near-black `#111111`; secondary text `#999999`; product tile background light grey `#F4F4F4`; accent deep indigo `#23234F`; occasional red accent `#D42B1E`.
- **Typography:** Logo in a high-contrast fashion serif (Italiana or Playfair Display), around 44px, tight tracking. Navigation in uppercase sans-serif (Noto Sans KR / Inter), 11px, medium weight, letter-spacing 0.14em, wide gaps between items. Editorial accents in italic serif (Playfair Display Italic). Body text 13px, line-height 1.9, mixing English and Korean.
- **Layout:** max-width 1600px container, 40px side padding, extremely airy vertical rhythm (80–112px section padding).

**Pages / sections (desktop, 1440px frame):**
1. **Header (sticky, white):** Large serif wordmark "M-LonG." top-left with small letter-spaced "OPTICAL" suffix. Below it, a nav row: BRAND · NEW SEASON · SUNGLASSES · OPTICAL · SPECIAL · COMMUNITY. Right side: globe icon, My / Login / Join / Cart (with black circular badge "0"), and a rectangular black-outlined search field.
2. **Hero:** Full-width campaign photo (82vh) — Korean model wearing white cat-eye sunglasses with orange mirrored lenses, red leather jacket, deep blue-violet studio background, cinematic moody light. Bottom-left overlay: italic serif line *"not to fit in, but to express me."* plus small tracked caption *"every moment feels true to who I am."* and signature *"Reality, M-LonG."* Bottom-right: thin divider line + "2026 SS · NEW SEASON".
3. **Brand section:** Centered portrait editorial photo (max width ~980px) — Korean model with white towel turban and translucent olive rectangular glasses on a light grey background. Below: bold small headline "Own your light.", a short Korean brand statement, a smaller grey English translation, and "@mlong_optical".
4. **Product grid "SUNGLASSES":** Left sidebar filter list in lowercase (all / essential / signature / premium / junior). 4-column grid of light-grey `#F4F4F4` square tiles, each with a clean front-view eyewear illustration (oval acetate black, oval tortoise, rectangle olive, rectangle black, round gold metal, round silver metal, cat-eye ivory, cat-eye deep red). Under each tile: product name (LUNE C1, ELVO C1, MOA C1, RHEA C1…), "+3 Colors" note right-aligned, original price struck through in grey "KRW 129,000", sale price "KRW 69,000" in black. Subtle zoom on hover.
5. **Special banner:** Full-width deep indigo `#23234F` block, centered: small tracked label "M-LONG.OPTICAL — SPECIAL", large italic serif headline "Essential is the new special.", a short Korean sub-line, and an outlined white "SHOP NOW" button that inverts on hover.
6. **Instagram strip:** 4 square tiles alternating campaign photo crops and eyewear product tiles on grey, hover zoom.
7. **Footer:** Serif wordmark left, INSTAGRAM / COMMUNITY / CONTACT links right, then tiny grey Korean-style business registration lines (business license, office address in Seoul, opening hours) and "Copyright © 2026 ©mlongoptical All right reserved."

**Interactions:** gentle hover states only (nav fades to grey, product image scales 1.04, banner button inverts). No shadows, no rounded cards — sharp, flat, editorial Korean minimalism.
